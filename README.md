# Giordano Alvari

**Senior AI / ML Engineer** working on production LLM systems, retrieval,
forecasting, and the engineering required to operate them reliably.

Currently at **Enel** in Florence, where I design and deliver AI applications
for operational and planning workflows across Italy and Spain. My work spans
technical architecture, backend implementation, model evaluation, deployment,
small-team coordination, and collaboration with operational and executive
stakeholders.

Before LLM systems, I worked on forecasting, predictive modelling, and MLOps
over distributed customer and energy data. I started in computational biology
at **DKFZ**, collaborating with EMBL on single-cell eQTL mapping; the work was
published in *Genome Biology*.
